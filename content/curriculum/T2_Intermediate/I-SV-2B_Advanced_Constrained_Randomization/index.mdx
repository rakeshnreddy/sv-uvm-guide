## Quick Take
- **What it is:** The next level of SystemVerilog constrained randomization, moving beyond basic `<` and `>` into conditional constraints (`->`, `if`/`else`), iterative constraints (`foreach`), and probability shaping (`dist`, `solve...before`, `soft`).
- **Why it matters:** Real-world protocols aren't just "address must be `<` 100". They involve dependent rules ("if it's a burst write, then the size must match the length"). You need advanced tools to model this.
- **The rule to carry:** constraints decide *which* combinations are legal. `dist` and `solve...before` decide *how often* each legal one appears. `soft` is a default with a rank, never a probability.

## Build Your Mental Model

Work through the five techniques and predict before each reveal. Notice three things. The payload array never makes long packets more likely, because sizes are solved before elements. The value `8` collects weight from *both* `dist` items. And `solve...before` changes `P(JUMBO)` without changing which pairs are legal. The last tab is the lab's bug in miniature: constraints that never fail, yet silently never produce IPV6.
