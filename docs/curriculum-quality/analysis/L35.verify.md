  - "Required dependencies: yes", "misconception 2: yes (Q5)" and "visualizer: yes (both instances)" are right.

  - "Channel signals: partial" is right; Table A3-1 pairs, ACLK/ARESETn and WID are all absent.
- **Core concepts missing from the concept table:**
  - The interface clock rule (§A3.1.1): sample on the rising edge, outputs change after it. It underlies every waveform and the driver idiom (V04).
  - Terminology (§A1.4.2): transaction, burst and beat, plus "transfer" and "handshake" (V05).
- **Practitioner gap:** the rules apply at every interface hop (§A1.3.2), so checker placement and interconnect W realignment follow from it (V15).
- **Expert layer.** The nine proposed items are sound and their quotes verify. Two additions:
  - In Expert 6, a counter checker gives no false positives (BVALID or RVALID cannot be legal with a count at or below `b_done`/`rlast_done`), only false negatives. Say so when the page states "necessary conditions only".
  - In Expert 1, a clocking-block drive issued right after `@(vif.cb)` takes effect at that same clocking event (IEEE 1800-2023 §14.16). This is why the analyst's `drive_aw` snippet does not hold AWVALID an extra cycle.
- **Debriefs.** All ten visualizer answers in L35.md:22-33 match my hand trace of the scenario specs (`axi-channel-model.ts:533-672`): edges 3, 2, 1, 4, 4 and the violation at 3; BVALID at 5; RLAST at 3; RVALID at 3; the legacy violation at 3.
