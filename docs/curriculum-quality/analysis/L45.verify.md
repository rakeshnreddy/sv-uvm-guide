## Summary

- **S1/S2 checked: 30.**
  - I-SV-8: 14 S2.
  - E-PWR-1: 1 S1 and 12 S2.
  - Lab: 3 S2.
- **Outcomes:** 26 confirmed, 4 adjusted, 0 refuted.
  - **The S1, L45-PWR-01 (the PH_TIMEOUT model), is confirmed** in both the LRM and uvm-core.
  - **L45-ISV8-02 is adjusted.** The finding stands, but the analyst's headline says the page uses commands "that no single edition accepts", and that is wrong. Every command on the page exists in 1801-2013, the edition its own `upf_version 2.1` declares: the split commands are *deprecated* there, and the PST and `set_domain_supply_net` are *legacy*.
  - **L45-ISV8-06 is adjusted.** The "every domain unpowered" rationale is UPF 1.0 semantics; 1801-2018 §1.3 allows abstract supply sets.
  - **L45-ISV8-10 is adjusted.** Do not hard-code "six simstates".
  - **L45-PWR-13 is adjusted.** Its correction is wrong for uvm-core: `stop_sequences()` does not drop the automatic objection.
  - **One S3 correction is also wrong.** L45-PWR-21's debrief says `save_after_off` keeps X out of PD_TOP; in the model it leaks X at step 6.
- **New issues: 19** (0 S1, 3 S2, 8 S3, 8 S4). The three S2s:
  - **L45-ISV8-V06.** The analyst's worked UPF, kata 2 and supply-network picture feed PD_CPU from VDD through `psw_cpu`, so VDD_CPU carries VDD's 1.2 V. Yet SS_CPU's ON state is written as `FULL_ON 0.8`. A switch does not change voltage, so that state never matches. The current page has the same contradiction.
  - **L45-PWR-V01.** E-PWR-1 L164 says stopping traffic lets the sequence drop its objection "normally". In uvm-core a stopped sequence skips `post_body`/`post_start`, and `stop_sequences()` leaks the automatic objection.
  - **L45-LAB-V01.** The lab's "correct" sleep path raises isolation and drops power in the same time step, which fails E-PWR-1's own `$fell(pwr) |-> $past(iso)` assertion.
- **Completeness gaps the analyst missed: 9.** Among them:
  - applying the UPF to a scope;
  - the X-into-always-on assertion;
  - the `stop_sequences` leak in the expert layer;
  - a supply/power/simstate layering picture;
  - how a second supply voltage enters the design;
  - async reset while clocks are gated;
  - the edition history (deprecated in 2013, gone in 2018);
  - the core lesson's elective prerequisite;
  - the depth-table entry "stopping stimulus", which should be "partial".
- **Verdict changes: none.**
  - I-SV-8 **rewrite**, E-PWR-1 **major** and the lab **rewrite (small)** follow the rules.
  - E-PWR-1 is borderline rewrite.
