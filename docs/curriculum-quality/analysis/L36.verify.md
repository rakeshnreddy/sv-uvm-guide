## Completeness and depth gaps the analyst missed

- **Must-cover.** The analyst's table is accurate. "Masters' 4KB handling" is partial (:259-266). The visualizer item is covered (:292; test pin at `amba-curriculum-content.spec.ts:30`).
- **Concept table.** Two cells are generous.
  - "WSTRB legality → 6 Picture: yes" depends on the learner finding the visualizer's strobe panel. No static picture shows a sparse strobe. The proposed W-channel TimingDiagram shows only full-lane strobes, so add one sparse beat, for example `4'b0110` on N = 2.
  - "Byte lanes → 4 Syntax: yes" lacks the DATA bit-slice and WSTRB[n] mapping (V04).
- **Core and practitioner gaps.**
  - The WRAP-on-boundary case (V03).
  - The data-bit mapping (V04).
  - The "either agent" bus-width rule (V05).
  - The "maximum bytes" reading of AxSIZE (V06).
- **Expert-layer additions.**
  - **Exclusive AxSIZE/AxLEN change.** A Non-modifiable exclusive access may have its AxSIZE and AxLEN changed if the total bytes stay the same (IHI0022 A4.3.1). This sharpens Expert 2 and Expert 3: a scoreboard must not compare exclusive bursts beat by beat.
  - **Non-modifiable splits.** A split Non-modifiable burst must keep every characteristic except burst length and the adapted address (A3.4.1). A checker can assert this on the far side of an interconnect.
- **Depth numbers.** All Expert 6 arithmetic holds. With all literals sized, the expression is 13 bits wide, and 256 × 128 = 32768 ≡ 0 mod 8192, so the illegal burst "passes". The IEEE clause numbers behind it are unverified here.
