## Completeness and depth gaps the analyst missed

**Spot-check of the analyst's tables.** Every "partial", "no" and "yes" I checked matches the files: the B-AXI-5 must-cover rows, objective 2 ("yes"), misconception 1 (tested by the Q1 distractor), and the B-AXI-6 objective rows. Two corrections:

- B-AXI-5, misconception 1: the analyst marks it "yes" from the Q1 distractor. The page also says so in prose (L19, L36, L44), so it is genuinely covered.

**Missing must-cover or expert items:**
1. **B-AXI-6, lane-aware comparison (practitioner).** The write path of the reference model must apply WSTRB as a byte mask. Read comparison must use only the active byte lanes of each beat (narrow and unaligned transfers), from B-AXI-2's `active_lanes()`. Without this, the scoreboard false-fails narrow traffic just as the X check does. Source: IHI0022E A3.4.1 (byte-lane equations), A3.4.3 (write strobes, narrow transfers). Add it to objective 2 and Make It Work, plus a mutant "wrong lane on a narrow read".
2. **B-AXI-6, reset handling (practitioner).** On ARESETn LOW, the monitor must flush its pending queues and the scoreboard its expected queues, or report them by policy. VALIDs are LOW during reset, and the earliest VALID comes after the first rising ACLK with ARESETn HIGH. Without this, leftovers cause false `check_phase` errors in reset-mid-test runs. Source: IHI0022E A3.1.2.
3. **B-AXI-5, the spec's explicit deadlock duty for interleaving slaves.** "To avoid possible deadlock, a slave interface that supports write data interleaving must continuously accept interleaved write data." The analyst puts it in expert item 3 as "AXI3 legacy". It should also appear in the core "Violation, legal policy, or contract" table, labelled AXI3-only, because it is the one READY wait that is *not* a free destination policy. Source: IHI0022E A5.3.3.

   Related: IHI0022E ties completion duties to deadlock explicitly in three more places, and the pitfall catalogue and expert item 5 should cite them:
   - A4.1, peripheral slaves: "Compliant completion of all transaction types is required to prevent system deadlock";
   - A10.2.4, same rule for undefined accesses;
   - B1.4.2: "A protection mechanism that discards transactions must provide a protocol-compliant error response to prevent deadlock".
4. **B-AXI-5 pitfall catalogue, add "read-sensitive devices and early termination".** A master that over-reads a FIFO peripheral loses data, because discarded beats still pop the FIFO. The spec requires the burst length to match exactly. Source: IHI0022E A3.4.1 Note ("When accessing such a device, a master must use a burst length that exactly matches the size of the required data transfer").
5. **B-AXI-5 kata and spine size.** The spine card asks for 6 scenarios and the analyst proposes 8; either is fine. The 8 answers are correct per A3.1.1, A3.2.1, A3.2.2, A3.3.1 and A3.4.4. Item 8 ("returns SLVERR on beat 3 of 8 and stops") should say "read burst", because a write has a single BRESP.
