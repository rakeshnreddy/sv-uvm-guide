## Completeness and depth gaps the analyst missed

1. **AHB burst addressing (B-AMBA-1, core).** Neither the Key Terminology section nor the target outline's AHB-Lite H3 says that each AHB beat has its own address phase (NONSEQ, then SEQ). Without this, the page's burst definition (L32-AMBA1-V01) stays wrong for AHB. Put one line in the AHB-Lite H3 and correct the terminology entry. Source: IHI0033B.b Table 3-1.
2. **AMBA 5 interface issues are missing from the family map.**
   - The "family, generation by generation" table stops at APB4, AHB5 and CHI.
   - The explorer cites IHI0024D "APB2 to APB5" (`amba-family-model.ts:6, 132`), but the analyst's "APB versions" expert item lists only APB2–APB4 (IHI0024C).
   - The table should name APB5 (IHI0024D, unverified here) and AXI5/ACE5 (later IHI0022 issues, unverified here) at awareness level. Otherwise the page and its own explorer disagree.
   - Keep the section numbers "unverified" until checked.
3. **ACE signal additions (B-AMBA-1, objective 4).** The coherent H3 and flashcard b-amba-1-11 should say that ACE changes the existing AR, AW and R channels as well as adding the snoop channels (IHI0022E §C1.3.1 Table C1-3). Testbench impact: the AR/AW monitors and the coverage gain DOMAIN, SNOOP and BAR fields.
4. **Per-channel VALID/READY picture (B-AMBA-2, objective 2).** See L32-AMBA2-V03.
5. **AXI interconnect picture on B-AMBA-2.** See L32-AMBA2-V04. It should be required, not optional.
6. **Write VALID-hold wording per channel (B-AMBA-2, practitioner).** §A3.2.2 states each channel's VALID rule as "must remain asserted until the rising clock edge after the slave asserts AWREADY" (and likewise for each channel). The proposed rule (c) is equivalent. The "how to read a waveform" steps should quote the per-channel form once, because learners meet it in B-AXI-1.
