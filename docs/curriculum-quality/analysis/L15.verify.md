## Completeness and depth gaps the analyst missed

**Must-cover: clock resolution (§16.16) and procedural assertions (§16.14.6).**
1. **Clocks carried by declarations.** §16.16 lists a named sequence or property with its own `@(…)`, and properties declared in a clocking block, among its sources. Rules b and f govern them. The outline, V7 and card i-sv-4a-13 omit them (L15-4A-04).
2. **Inference condition c (§16.14.6).** Exactly one edge expression may qualify, and its signal must not be used elsewhere in the body.
