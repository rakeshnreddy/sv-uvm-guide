## Findings

### S4

**N1, S4. advanced-constraints.mdx:883: "INCR allows 16 lengths at any address" overstates the INCR space.**
- **Problem.** R5 (`c_page`) also limits INCR addresses: the low byte plus the burst bytes must be at most 256. The percentages that follow are correct, because they come from an enumeration that includes R5, but the qualitative sentence is looser than the model.
- **Fix.** Write "INCR allows 16 lengths at any address that keeps the burst in its page; FIXED allows 4 lengths; WRAP allows 4 lengths and only aligned addresses".
- **Source.** The page's own R5; Python enumeration (70.7 / 18.7 / 10.6 %).
