## Findings

### S3

1. **S3 index.mdx:1079-1222: the key is the longest option in 12 of 13 questions.** Q3 is the only exception.
   - **Problem.** Measured lengths:
     - Q7: key 159 characters vs distractors 39-83;
     - Q13: 156 vs 36-64;
     - Q8: 125 vs 56-83;
     - Q12: 120 vs 24-55;
     - Q9: 121 vs 39-52;
     - Q6: 108 vs 26-61.

     The key positions are well spread (3,2,3,1,4,2,4,1,3,2,4,1,2). But `Quiz.tsx` does not shuffle the options, so a test-wise learner can score most of the quiz by picking the longest option. The sibling review L27b finding 1 rated the same pattern S3.
   - **Fix.** Move the "why" out of each key into its `feedback`. Examples:
     - Q7: "read() returns stale request data; the mirror holds the DUT value";
     - Q8: "Bit-bash wrote 1 to the GO bit";
     - Q9: "Nowhere";
     - Q11: "INDEX's and DATA's only";
     - Q12: "A post_predict callback that forces 0 on writes";
     - Q13: "include_coverage() ran too late".

     Lengthen one or two distractors per question so that the key is not the longest in more than about a third of the items.
   - **Source.** Spine §5.5 ("the answer must not be guessable"); `src/components/ui/Quiz.tsx` (no shuffle); L27b.completeness.r1 finding 1.

2. **S3 index.mdx:13-18 and :1157-1167: the objective list drops card objective 3, and Q8 is tagged to an objective it does not test.**
   - **Problem.** The index is the module page, and its objective list leaves out card objective 3 (run the built-in sequences and exclude unsafe registers) and the backdoor half of card objective 2. Yet Q8, which tests misconception 4 (built-in sequences and unsafe registers), is tagged `objective: 3`. Objective 3 is "Debug a mirror mismatch from its log", and Q8 has neither a mirror mismatch nor a log. The `objective` traceability is therefore wrong for Q8, and a learner who reads the objectives cannot see that the module, through its sub-lessons, also delivers the built-in-sequence and backdoor competencies.
   - **Fix (either):**
     - (a) Add one line after the objective list: "The sub-lessons add: predict the mirror after backdoor accesses ([Frontdoor vs. Backdoor](…)) and run the built-in register tests safely ([Built-in RAL Sequences](…))." Then broaden objective 3 to "Debug a mirror mismatch or a failing register test from its log", so that Q8 fits.
     - (b) Rewrite Q8 as a log-based item. For example: "a DMA-started error appears during `uvm_reg_bit_bash_seq`; the log shows a write of 1 to CMD.GO at …".
   - **Source.** Spine §5.4 (module objectives are taken from the card; every quiz item carries an `objective`); spine card A-UVM-4B objectives 2 and 3.

3. **S3 index.mdx:15, :125: objective 2 names RC, but nothing on the page exercises RC prediction.**
   - **Problem.** Objective 2 says the learner will "Predict the mirrored value after … side-effect policies (W1C, W1T, RC)". RC appears only in one row of the policy table ("A predicted frontdoor read sets the mirror to 0"). There is no traced example, quiz item, card or kata step for RC. W1C has a traced table, Q4 and a card; W1T has Q3, an interview item and kata mutant C. The card's objective 2 explicitly lists "side-effect accesses (W1C, RC)".
   - **Fix (either):**
     - Add one predict item or card. For example: "RX_STATUS.OVF is RC; the DUT holds 1 and an explicit predictor is connected. The test calls `read()`. What do `read()`, the DUT and the mirror hold, and does a following `mirror(UVM_CHECK)` pass?" Key: 1, 0, 0; the check passes. Use another register name, so that INT_STATUS.ERR keeps one policy (V17).
     - Or drop RC from objective 2 and point to Frontdoor vs. Backdoor for RC.
   - **Source.** Spine §5.4 (every objective exercised); spine card objective 2; L27.verify.md V17.
