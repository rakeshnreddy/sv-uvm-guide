## Requests for the lead (kit, not blocking this group)

1. **BlockDiagram on phones.** `minWidth = min(width, 360)` makes any three-column ArchitectureDiagram draw at about 0.54× on a 390 px screen. A minimum rendered font size, or letting wide diagrams scroll at full size instead of shrinking, would fix this curriculum-wide (V1, V2).
2. **Edge label chips** are drawn over the line, so any label wider than its gap hides the edge (V1, V2; also seen in other groups). Offsetting the chip above horizontal lines, or warning in `layoutGridDiagram` when `6.2 × chars + 8` exceeds the line length, would catch it at authoring time.
3. **CycleWaveform markers** (V10): visible label text or an sr-only marker list, and a neutral don't-care hatch.
4. Carried over from the author: the `AxiIdOrderingVisualizer` "read after write, same ID" preset and `initialPreset` prop. Not an S2 here: Rule 4 already has a waveform and a sequence diagram.
