### S3-1. The FIFO is attributed to the I-UVM-3C lesson, but only its lab uses it
- **Where:** `index.mdx:40`, `:270`, `:430`, `:434`, `:592`, `:682`, `:1136`, `:1251`.
- **Problem:** The page says "the FIFO you met in I-UVM-3C", "the I-UVM-3C monitor, which samples `dout` with `#0`", "the capstone's traffic", and "Now the structure of I-UVM-3C, in Python". The I-UVM-3C lesson body builds a `mem_*` bench (`mem_item`, `mem_if`, `mem_sb`). `packet_fifo`, its 7-write/7-read traffic, the `INJECT_FIFO_BUG` define and the `SCB_SUMMARY matches=7` totals exist only in that lesson's lab `uvm-mini-capstone` (I-UVM-3C `:1595-1599`; `labs/uvm_capstone/lab1_fifo_env/solution.sv:1-11`). A learner who read I-UVM-3C but skipped the lab has not met this FIFO, so the "port" framing and the side-by-side comparison lose their anchor. The analysis also asked for lab `uvm-mini-capstone` under Related (analysis Navigation), and it is missing.
- **Fix:** Say "the FIFO from I-UVM-3C's capstone lab (`uvm-mini-capstone`)" at `:40` and `:430`, and "the capstone lab's monitor" at `:270`. Add `<LabLink labId="uvm-mini-capstone" />` (or a Related bullet naming the lab) so the SV source of the port is one click away. At `:682` say "the architecture of I-UVM-3C and its capstone lab".
- **Source checked:** I-UVM-3C `index.mdx:16`, `:292`, `:1595-1599`; `solution.sv:1-11`, `:158-195`, `:497-520`; analysis L47 Hands-on plan and Navigation.

### S3-3. The combinational-READY caveat sends practitioners to the wrong pattern
- **Where:** `index.mdx:262`.
- **Problem:** "A READY that is combinational from VALID needs `await ReadWrite()` after the edge instead." In the page's own pattern, VALID is written just after the rising edge and applied at that time step's ReadWrite point. By `FallingEdge` + `ReadOnly`, a READY that is combinational from VALID and registered state has already settled, so the falling-edge read is still correct. The sentence implies that the pattern breaks for a common class of slaves, and it points at an unnamed "pattern" in the cocotb docs with no example. The real limit is a READY that changes between the falling edge and the next rising edge, for example because it depends on another input written later in the cycle.
- **Fix:** Either delete the sentence, or state the real precondition: "This works as long as READY settles before the falling edge, which holds for a registered READY and for a READY that is combinational from VALID and registered state." Ask the accuracy reviewer to confirm.
- **Source checked:** this page's timing table `:109-115` and its write-scheduling statement `:105` (cocotb 2.1 `handle.py` `_schedule_write`); cocotb Timing Model five phases.
