## Findings

### S2

**S2-1. The key is always option 1 (8 of 8 quiz questions).**
- **Where:** `mailboxes.mdx:788, :799, :810, :821, :832, :843, :854, :865` (the `correct: true` answers of Q1–Q8).
- **Problem.**
  - `Quiz.tsx` renders `answers` in authored order and does not shuffle. On this page the right answer is therefore always the first button. A learner sees the pattern by Q2 or Q3, and from then on the quiz no longer tests objectives 1–4 and the misconception distractors (including card misconception 3) are never weighed.
  - A second cue: the key is clearly the longest option in Q4 (116 characters vs at most 97), Q7 (116 vs at most 103) and Q8 (111 vs at most 87). The author log's claim that "distractor lengths are balanced, so the key cannot be guessed by length" holds for Q1–Q3 and Q5–Q6 only.
  - The sibling `events.mdx` spreads its keys over positions 1–4 (L17b review), and L03's round-1 review rated the same all-position-1 pattern S2, so this is not the program norm.
- **Fix.**
  - Reorder the answer objects so the keys spread evenly, for example Q1–Q8 → positions 3, 2, 4, 1, 2, 4, 3, 1. Keep each `correct` flag on its option. No feedback or explanation refers to a position, so nothing else changes. The two IQPs (both key 2) can stay.
  - Shorten the Q4, Q7 and Q8 keys, or lengthen one distractor in each, so that the key is not the longest option.
  - No spec pins these options (`grep` of `tests/`).
- **Sources checked:** spine §5.5 ("the answer must not be guessable"; the per-option `correct` flag exists so that reordering cannot break the key); README rubric R6; `src/components/ui/Quiz.tsx` (`toFormatted`, `options.map`); scratch parse of the quiz; `reviews/L03.completeness.r1.md` S2-1 and `reviews/L17b.completeness.r1.md` for precedent.
