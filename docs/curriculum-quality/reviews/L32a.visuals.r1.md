## Notes for the lead (no action needed for pass)

- In the SoC diagram, the three master links meet at the interconnect's top-centre and the three slave links leave from its bottom-centre, because the kit anchors at side centres. This reads a little like a shared-bus junction on a page that says AXI is a point-to-point *interface*. It is acceptable for a structural sketch and is not raised as a finding. Spreading anchors along a side, or a neutral port kind, in the kit would let authors avoid it. The same limitation was noted in L32b.visuals.r1.
- `layoutGridDiagram` reports node overlap only. Both diagrams here pass it and still have hidden labels and lines. A label-box check (label vs label, label vs node, label longer than edge) in `layoutGridDiagram` or `check-mdx` would catch findings 1 and 2 for every page.
- `BlockDiagram` exposes its SVG as `role="group"` with an `aria-label`, whereas README l.133 asks for `role="img"`. This is kit-level and not raised against the page.
- The author's lead requests 1–4 (frontmatter order/tier, spine Appendix A citations, model citation IHI0024D vs C/E, L32b AHB wording) are outside this lens.
