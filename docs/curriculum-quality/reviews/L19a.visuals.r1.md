### L19a-VIS-01 · S3 · `index.mdx:303-316` (picture 3, "The same bench in UVM roles")
- **Problem.** Three label collisions make the central mechanisms picture partly unreadable. I confirmed them in the live render at 1100 px.

  3. The `cov.imp` port label "analysis_export" (`:307`) is anchored inside `cov` at the node's centre line and overprints the node label "cov".
- **Fix.** Any of the following, or a combination:
  - Remove the label of the `mon.ap → cov.imp` edge (the caption already says both receive every write).
  - Shorten the driver edge label to "pull" and leave `get_next_item` / `item_done` to the caption, or widen the gap with `gapX={170}`.
  - Put the `cov` port on the top side (`side: "top"`) or shorten its label to "export". Check the result again in the live page.
