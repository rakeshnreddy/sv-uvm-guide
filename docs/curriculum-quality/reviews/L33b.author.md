- **Content differences between B.b and C** that the page relies on, all verified:
  - The §5.1.2 Note in B.b recommends at most 16 wait states and exempts a serial boot ROM. C drops both and keeps only the predetermined maximum.
  - IDLE address alignment is a rule in B.b §3.5 and a recommendation in C §3.6.
  - C §3.3 defines when the bus is locked or unlocked. The lock counter uses that definition.
  - C §4.4 says the interconnect may stall a master with HREADY while it waits for arbitration.
  - C §5.1.3 says a master that receives ERROR on a read may still use the data, and recommends driving HRDATA to zero.
  - C §8.2 lists the signals that must always be valid.
- **SystemVerilog.** IEEE 1800-2023 (`scratchpad/audit/t1_lrm_clean.txt`): §9.2.2.4, §16.9.3 (`$past` with a gating expression; `$sampled` in action blocks), §16.14.1, §16.14.3, §16.14.8 and §16.15 (`default disable iff`, and the LRM example of `disable iff (1'b0)`).
