| 1 | **S2** | parameterized-classes.mdx:105 | "`bf.push("hello")` or storing `bf.pop()` into a `string` would not compile". A string literal is an unsigned integral constant, and assigning it to an integral type is legal: it is truncated to the rightmost byte (`"o"`, 8'h6F), at most with a tool warning.
