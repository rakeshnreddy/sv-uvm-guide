## Verified as correct (no action)

- **Model syntax.** These forms match the PSS 3.0 BNF: `pool [2] dma_chan_r chan_p;`, `bind chan_p *;`, `lock dma_chan_r chan;`, `constraint { … }`, the single-item `constraint bursts in [4..32];`, `c0: do dma_subsys_c::dma_copy_a;`, `rand bit[32]`, the `[a..b]` ranges and the mustache `{{chan.instance_id}}`. It is a scalar attribute, and §22.5.3 allows `s1.b`-style references.
- **Pool [1] prediction** (lines 268, Q7, card 7). Example 65 in §12.3.4 states that a single-instance pool would create a dependency that "would violate the rules of the parallel statement"; §14.2 agrees. The Q7 distractor feedback is right.
- **Expected output.**
  - `0x20000000` = 536870912 and `0x80000400` = 2147484672.
  - `bit` prints as unsigned decimal (§22.5.3.2).
  - Both solutions satisfy every constraint: alignment, SRAM bound, the windows, and `len` ranges with 64-byte multiples.
- **The SoC SystemVerilog-target failure.** The pitfall at line 171, debug row 307, card 4 and the checklist match uvm-core. `start(null, this)` takes the parent's sequencer, a `p_sequencer` cast gives `DCLPSQ`, and without a driver `start_item` blocks until `PH_TIMEOUT`. `p_sequencer.apb_sqr` is a null member of a non-null handle, so there is no null dereference.
- **Executors.** The §24.6 quotes, `executor_group_c`, `executor_claim_s`, "strictly a test-realization artifact" (said of the executor and group components), and the "free to determine" and "responsible for employing the means" quotes all match the text.
- **Other claims.**

  - §22.8 and Table 25: the three flows.
  - Clauses 18 and 19, with behavioral coverage new in 3.0.
  - §24.7: regions with traits such as DRAM and SRAM.
  - §24.11: `reg_c` and register groups.
  - §5.2.2: default pool size 1.
- **Quiz keys Q1–Q7.** Each has exactly one correct answer and is consistent with the sources. Q2's "2.1 approved October 2023" matches the 3.0 front matter.
- **Flashcards 1–14.** All are accurate. Cards 13 and 14 match the index's RAL-prediction and passive-scoreboard teaching.
