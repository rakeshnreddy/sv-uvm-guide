- **Timeline.**
  - Rising edges fall at 5, 15, … `por_n` is released at 20, and `##1` resumes at 25.
  - The A5/3C/load drive lands in Re-NBA at 25, so `cpu_core` loads at 35, and the `$strobe` at 35 prints a5/3c.
  - `sleep_req` is driven at 55 and sampled at 65 (CLK_OFF). The steps follow at 75, 85, 95 and 105.
  - `wake_req` is driven at 145 and sampled at 155 (PWR_ON). The steps follow at 165 to 205. RUN is at 215 and `$finish` at 235.

  - Both expected logs match line by line. In run 2: the clamp from 75; X at 105 and 155; 00 at 165; a5 at 185 (leading edge of the level-sensitive restore, per Balasubramanian); release at 215.
- **Time 0.** The race on `negedge por_n` at time 0 is harmless: the PMU and `cpu_core` both reset at edge 5, while `por_n` = 0.
