## Notes for the lead (kit level; no page change requested)

- **Self-message labels cross the next lifeline.** `layoutSequence` wraps self labels at 24 mono characters, which is about 150–165 px including the step number. They start 42 px right of the lifeline, so any `columnWidth` below about 210 px puts them across the next lifeline. Here steps 2 and 4 cross the `apb_mon` and `dma` lifelines (seen in the render). They stay legible over the dashed line. A real fix would derive the wrap width from `columnWidth`.
- **Phone legibility of wide architecture figures.** `ArchitectureDiagram` sets `minWidth = min(width, 360)`. The 716 px platform figure therefore renders at about half scale at 390 px, so its 9.5 px sublabels appear at about 4.8 px. This affects every four-column kit figure in the curriculum, so it belongs in the phase-6 390 px sweep. A larger minimum width (scroll instead of shrink) for wide figures would fix it.
- **Generated navigation data is stale.** `src/lib/curriculum-data.tsx:1564–1566` still titles this page "Portable Stimulus (PSS)", with the old description (author's lead request 4: run `generate:curriculum`).
- **Outside this lens.** Accuracy r2's other two S4 wording items are still unchanged in the working tree: :39 ("the one piece of syntax on this page") and :318 ("stays above 0x800FF000").
