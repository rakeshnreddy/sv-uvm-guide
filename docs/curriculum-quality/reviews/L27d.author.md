## Expert items added (with sources)

1. **IEEE 1800.2-2020 versus uvm-core 2020.3.1 in Annex E**: E.1 "resets the DUT" vs empty `reset_blk()`; E.8.1 `virtual class` vs concrete factory-registered class; E.2.1 "mirrored value" vs `get()`; E.7 "warning" vs `uvm_error`; E.5.1 lists `NO_MEM_SHARED_ACCESS_TEST` vs code's `NO_MEM_ACCESS_TEST`; `uvm_mem_walk_seq` block-level check reads `NO_MEM_ACCESS_TEST`; memory/shared/HDL-path sequences revisit nested sub-blocks (`get_blocks()` default `UVM_HIER` plus recursion). Sources: Annex E text; `src/reg/sequences/*.svh`; `uvm_reg_block.svh` `get_blocks`.
2. **Extending the built-in sequences**: `reset_blk()` override (code), factory overrides of the single sequences (created with `type_id::create`), `uvm_reg_mem_access_seq` uses `new()` so overrides do not reach it, `do_block()`. Source: `src/reg/sequences/*.svh`.
3. **Cost and scale**: accesses per element per map for every sequence, worked numbers (200 registers, 64K-word memory), tool-dependent labelling of emulator/gate-level cost. Source: the loops in `src/reg/sequences/*.svh`.
4. **Making a false pass loud**: report catcher matching message text (§6.6), with the reason not to promote the shared ID; counting "Verifying" lines; a single exclusion function. Sources: §6.6, §F.3.3.1, `uvm_reg_access_seq.svh`.
5. **Interview angles**: three attempt-first `InterviewQuestionPlayground` items (false pass, SoC strategy, intermittent bit-bash failure).
