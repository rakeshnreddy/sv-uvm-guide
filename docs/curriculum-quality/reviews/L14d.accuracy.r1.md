## Findings

### S3-1: Kata 1 rows 3 and 5 contradict each other
- **Location:** `closure-workflow.mdx:772` (row 3), `:774` (row 5) and `:784` (solution row 3).
- **Problem:** Row 3 says that apart from the DIV cells, "7 of the other 12 cells are hit. The empty ones differ from seed to seed", and the solution classifies them all as rare. Row 5 says that `<MUL, a=00>` and `<AND, a=00>` are empty because the monitor drops every `y == 0` transaction. Both cells are among row 3's 12 cells, and with that filter they are empty on every seed. Row 3's "differ from seed to seed" is therefore false for two of its five empty cells, and its "rare" classification is wrong for them. A careful learner gets two answers for the same cells.
- **Fix:** Make the rows disjoint. Either:
  - row 3 becomes "apart from the two DIV cells and the two cells in row 5, 7 of the other 10 cells are hit…", with the solution saying "rare, except the two row-5 cells"; or
  - row 5 moves to a different cross or block, so the filter does not touch `x_op_a` edge cells.
- **Source:** internal consistency, using the page's own `alu_model` (0 * b = 0 and 0 & b = 0).

### S4-1: Quiz Q6 rounds 0.0347% to "About 0.04%"
- **Location:** `closure-workflow.mdx:701`.
- **Problem:** The keyed answer says "About 0.04%", and its own feedback says "exactly 0.035%". The exact value is 0.0347%, which is 0.03% to one significant figure.
- **Fix:** Use "About 0.035%" (or "About 0.03%").
- **Source:** exact inclusion–exclusion (Python).

### S4-2: Flashcard 10 and the "exactly" wording at `:176` mix the estimate with the exact value
- **Location:** `content/flashcards/I-SV-3B_Closure_Workflow.json` (card `isv3b-cw-10`).
- **Problem:** The card says ln(k/δ)/p "for x_op_a … is about 10,050 … steered … about 155". Those are the exact values; the formula itself gives 10,098 and 158. The page body (`:181`, `:584`) gets this right ("about 10,100; the exact answer is 10,053"). Separately, `:176` says "exactly ln 20 / p for small p", which contradicts itself: the exact form is ln 20 / −ln(1 − p).
- **Fix:**
  - Card 10: "…gives about 10,100 (exact: 10,053) … about 158 (exact: 155)".
  - `:176`: "about ln 20 / p".
- **Source:** arithmetic.

### S4-3: Optional precision notes (no change required)
- **Steering table and the Make It Work bench.** `:146` calls the steering-table numbers "exact" for a generator that also carries the ALU-5 constraint `op == DIV -> b != 0` in Make It Work. Under §18.5.9 that constraint makes the cells 1/1791 (non-DIV) and about 1/1798 (DIV), not 1/1792. The closure probability is unchanged to three significant figures (0.0347%), and the debrief at `:536` already says "p ≈ 1/1792". A short note "(ignoring the b ≠ 0 rule, which changes nothing at this precision)" would make "exact" literally true.
- **Merging.** `:200` could mention that §40.3.2.4 `$coverage_merge` exists but leaves the format and the algorithm to the implementation. The current wording is not wrong.
- **IQP 2.** `:621`: with `at_least = 2` and one hit per seed, a per-seed report shows the cell as uncovered with 1 hit, not "empty". Consider "shows it uncovered".
