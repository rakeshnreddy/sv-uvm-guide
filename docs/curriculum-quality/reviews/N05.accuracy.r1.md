## Hand re-trace of the bench (independent of the author)

- **Power-on reset.** `rst_n` is 0 at edges 5 and 15 and 1 from 25. The driver leaves its wait at 25, so the first request is accepted at 35. The monitor starts with `in_reset = 1`, so the power-on reset publishes no `MON_RESET`.
- **Traffic before the reset.**
  - The 16 writes are accepted from 35 to 185 ns; the reads are accepted at 195 + 10a ns. The read of address 3 is accepted at 225 ns and the read of 4 at 235 ns, and the read of 5 is on the bus at 245 ns.
  - The vseq's 24th `@(rst_vif.cb)` is at 235 ns. Its drive lands in the Re-NBA region of 235 ns, so `rst_n` is sampled 0 at 245, 255 and 265 ns and 1 from 275 ns.
- **TimingDiagram rows.** I checked every row against this trace. For example, `rdata` is 2D/3C/4B at 225/235/245 ns ({a, ~a}) and 00 from 255 ns.
- **The reset itself.**
  - At 245 ns the driver releases the read of 5 with `item_done()`, so the FIFO is popped, and `traffic` then waits in `start_item`.
  - The monitor discards the read of 4 and the scoreboard flushes.
  - At 255 ns `stop_sequences()` kills `traffic`. The request FIFO is empty, so there is no extra INFO line. Inside `start()`, `m_killing_process` is already null and the state is UVM_STOPPED, so there is no zombie warning.
- **The error sequence.**
  - The first request of `errs` is driven at 275 ns and accepted at 285 ns. Its good parity is ^{1,0010,10100101} = 0, so par = 1 and `MEM_PARITY` is reported at 285 ns.
  - The read of address 2 is accepted at 295 ns and returns 00, a match. The bad-parity read of address 3 (good parity 0) is accepted at 305 ns, which raises `MEM_PARITY` at 305 ns.
- **End of the run.** `again` is accepted from 315 to 625 ns, the objection drops at 625 ns and the drain ends at 645 ns.
- **Counts.** There are 21 matches (4 + 1 + 16). There are 14 INFO reports, and the catcher summary is counted because it is executed before the composition. The ID list is in sorted order.
- **BUG=1 run.** It ends with `SCB_MISMATCH` (`a5` against `00`) at 305 ns, after the monitor's `MEM_PARITY` in the same time slot. The FIFO wake-up happens after the monitor suspends, so that order is correct.
