| L05c-A1 | S3 | ipc.mdx:221 (also :195, :322; `F2D_IPC.json:50`, f2d-ipc-10) | "An early `return`, a `disable` or a `disable fork` inside the critical section skips `put()`." A `disable fork` executed by the key-holding process ends only its *descendants*, so the holder itself still reaches `put()`.
