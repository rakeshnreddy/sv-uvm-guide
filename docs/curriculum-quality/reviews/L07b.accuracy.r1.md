## Traces

**Worked example.**
- Clock: rising edges at 5+10k ns.
- `idle()` at 0 ns is not on a clocking event, so it acts at 5 ns (§14.16).
- Reset is released at 20 ns (a negedge). The slave holds `ready` at 0 through 15 ns, then toggles: 1 after 25, 0 after 35, 1 after 45, 0 after 55, 1 after 65, 0 after 75.
- `run()` starts at 30 ns. The first `write` resumes on `@(cb)` at 35 and drives in Re-NBA. At 45, `cb.ready` = 0 (keep waiting). At 55, `cb.ready` = 1: exit, and `valid <= 0`.
  - The monitor's Active-region read at 55 sees valid = 1 and ready = 1, and prints `t=55 … 00001000 … a5a50001`.
- The second `write`: `@(cb)` at 65, drive, `cb.ready` = 1 at 75, and the monitor prints `t=75 … 00001004 … a5a50002`.
- Negedges at 80 and 90, then `PASS=2 FAIL=0` and `$finish` at 90 ns.
- **Matches** :550-554, :578-584, the SequenceDiagram and the TimingDiagram.

**Debug cases.**
- **Case 1:** fatal at 0 ns in `idle()`.
- **Case 5:**
  - With `!==`, `write` hangs and the watchdog fires at 1 us.
  - With `while (!cb.ready)`, the transfers are skipped and the check prints PASS=0 FAIL=3: 2 misses plus the size mismatch.
- **Cases 2-4** are static errors for the stated reasons (§23.3.3.2, §25.10/§25.5, §25.9 PBus "selected modport to no selected modport: illegal").

**Debug challenge.**
- **Broken:** `ready <= valid` and the master waits for READY first, so both stay at 0: a deadlock.
- **Fixed:** valid is up after 35. The slave sets ready after 45, `cb.ready` = 1 at 55, and the first transfer prints at 55. valid drops at 55, so the slave sets ready to 0 at 65. `write` 2 drives at 65, the slave sets ready to 1 at 75, `cb.ready` = 1 at 85, and the second transfer prints at 85. Negedges at 90 and 100, then PASS=2 at 100 ns.
- **Matches** :1024.

**Kata solution.**
- The modport mixes `input`, `output` and `import`, which the syntax allows.
- The task samples on posedge and drives on negedge. The `!==` loop is x-safe.
- The DUT write to `bus.addr` is illegal through `.slave`.
