- **Names against the code.**
  - ArchitectureDiagram: `vsqr`, `bus_sqr`, `pmu_sqr`, `mon.ap`, `mon.req_ap`, `mon.rsp_ap`, `analysis_export`, `req_export`, `rsp_export`, `pwr_export`, `u_cpu_pd (cpu_pd_model)` and `u_chk` all match `pwr_env::connect_phase`, the agents, `pwr_scoreboard`, `pwr_cov` and `tb_top`.

- **Timing values, hand-traced from the code** (clock edges at 5, 15, ... ns; `ACK_DELAY` = 3; clocking drives take effect after the edge):
  - **PWR_OFF waveform.** `cpu_pwr_en` is driven at edge 0 (205 ns) and sampled 0 from edge 1. The switch counts at edges 1 to 3 and the acknowledge falls after edge 3. `ctx_reg` is X when sampled at edge 4 (245 ns), which is also when `pmu_drv` sees the acknowledge and `pmu_mon` publishes PWR_OFF. The `a_iso_before_off` pass at edge 1 is correct.
  - **"Isolation released before the restore" waveform.** It is consistent with `pwr_seq_checker`'s flag logic: `reset_seen` is set from edge 2 and `restored` from edge 7. The fail at edge 5 is consistent too: `cpu_clk_en` is already 1 and `restored` is 0. So is the order of PMU drives that the waveform implies (RST, CLK_ON, ISO_OFF, RESTORE).
  - **Scoreboard trace table.** The times are 55/65 ns, 85–155 ns, ISO_ON published at 185 ns, PWR_OFF at 245 ns, PWR_ON/RST/RESTORE/CLK_ON at 355/395/405/425 ns, ISO_OFF at 435 ns, the post-wake reads at 445–485 ns, the random items at 505–605 ns, and the end at 625 ns with the 20 ns drain. All of them agree with `pmu_drv::do_op`, `pmu_mon`, `bus_drv` and `tb_top`.
  - **Logs.** The Expected output, "Prove it can fail" (405 and 455 ns) and the Kata 1 log are consistent with the same trace.
  - **Coverage figures.** 100.00% for the normal run. 66.67% for the skip-RST mutation, because the power-on RST keeps `cp_op` at 100% and only `cp_up` misses.
- **Visualizers.**
