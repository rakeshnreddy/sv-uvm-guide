## Notes for the lead (integration and kit; not author fixes)

1. **Stale lab registry (S3 for learners until regenerated).** `src/generated/lab-registry.ts` still holds `soc-vip-reuse` version 1 (from :1632). Its steps say "`BUS_COLLISION` fatal" and `"env.spi_agt"`, which contradict the version 2 lab.json, README and starter (`APB_X`, `"env.spi_env.spi_agent"`, five steps). The capstone is also still version 1. `validate-content-manifests.mjs` fails on this. Run `node scripts/generate-lab-registry.mjs` at integration (author lead request 1).
2. **Kit: nested containers that share a top row.** `layoutGridDiagram` adds `CONTAINER_MARGIN` (12) per nesting level, but the label band is `CONTAINER_LABEL` (18). An inner container that starts on the same row as its parent therefore draws its top border through the parent's label. The kit's own example in `visual-language.md` §9 (`env` and `agent` both at row 0) has the same geometry. Suggested fix: grow the top offset by `CONTAINER_MARGIN + CONTAINER_LABEL` per level of inner depth, keeping the side and bottom margins as they are. V-01's author workaround is not needed once this lands.
3. **Kit: edges into one node converge at its centre.** In the RAL diagram (:378-380) `cpu_predictor`, `dbg_predictor` and auto-predict all end at `soc_rm`'s top centre, so their three arrowheads overlap. `nodeAnchor` always uses the centre of the facing side. An optional `toAt`/`fromAt` offset on `GridEdge` would let authors separate them without adding port glyphs. Polish only; the caption names all three paths.
4. **Phone width (kit behaviour, phase-6 sweep).** The 642-682-unit diagrams (traceability :119, RAL :354, regression :628) render node sublabels at about 5 px at 390 px. Every kit figure also scrolls about 20 px inside its frame, because `minWidth` is 360 and the frame has 340 px. The page does not overflow, and the captions and tables carry the content.
