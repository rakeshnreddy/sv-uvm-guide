| V1 | S3 | index.mdx:156 (and Recap line 395) | The debrief of "The life of one `uvm_error" says that only two things after the verbosity check can remove an error from the counts: UVM_NO_ACTION and a catcher. But the diagram's own step 3 (line 146, "apply severity override") can turn a `UVM_ERROR` into a `UVM_WARNING` or `UVM_INFO` before counting. `+uvm_set_severity=...,_ALL_,UVM_ERROR,UVM_INFO` therefore takes errors out of `UVM_ERROR :` and gives a false pass. The page covers this elsewhere (line 313, line 671 and interview angle 1), but this debrief, and the Recap bullet at line 395 ("only `UVM_NO_ACTION` and catchers remove a report from the counts"), state the opposite in absolute terms. | Reword: "...remove an error from the error count: a severity override in the handler (it is then counted at its new severity), an action of UVM_NO_ACTION, and a report catcher." | uvm-core `uvm_report_handler.svh` 371–388; IEEE 1800.2-2020 §6.3.7, §6.4.6 |
| V2 | S3 | index.mdx:235 and 243 | The page tells learners to leave the **Report catcher** knob on `none` because catchers belong to E-DBG-1. A learner who clicks the presets lands in out-of-scope content, and the debrief does not cover it. | Add "and skip the two catcher presets" to the instruction at line 235.
