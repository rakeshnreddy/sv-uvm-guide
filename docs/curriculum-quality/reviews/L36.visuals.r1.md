## Accessibility and theme

- The four figure SVGs use `role="img"` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at a `<title>` and `<desc>` (unique `baxi2-*` ids), and each has a `figcaption`. The recap SVG uses `aria-label`. The diagram-kit figures take `title` (accessible name) and `caption`, and CycleWaveform adds a screen-reader table of sampled values.
- No hard-coded colour: text and lines use `currentColor`; fills use Tailwind theme classes (`fill-cyan-500/15`, `stroke-cyan-600 dark:stroke-cyan-400`, amber, rose); `tailwind.config.ts` scans `./content/**/*.mdx` with `darkMode: "class"`. A grep for hex, rgb, white or black on the page finds nothing.
- Not colour-only: legal/illegal carry ✓/✕ and words; inactive lanes are dashed with "-"; the amber cells are also labelled (0x1000, "N = 4", "1", "A"); the over-the-fence cell is dashed and labelled "past the fence".
- Rendered at 390 px in light and dark (Chromium and WebKit): every label is legible and nothing is clipped. Problems found are polish (V5-V8).
