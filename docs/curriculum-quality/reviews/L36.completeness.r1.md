## Depth ladder

- **Core.** Context comes before mechanics: one address per burst, then the fields, the equations, WRAP, FIXED, lanes and WSTRB. Terms are mostly defined before use: transfer and beat, byte lane, aligned, cache line, FIFO, narrow and unaligned, page, interconnect, DMA engine. The analogy states where it breaks. But the "How to read this lesson" note leaves the 4KB rule, the pitfalls and the recap out of the core path (F4).
- **Practitioner.** Make It Work has:
  - a `// compile` package and `tb_top`;
  - an Expected output block, labelled computed;
  - a "Run it" box;
  - a five-rule legality checker;
  - UVM placement: item, driver, monitor snippet, scoreboard, coverage;
  - a 7-row symptom → cause → fix table;
  - a numbered how-to and a `- [ ]` checklist.
- **Expert.** There are 7 `### Expert:` items, well above the minimum of 3 for an index page:
  - 6 cite IHI0022E or IEEE 1800-2023 sections;
  - the interconnect-transformation and solver-speed claims are labelled implementation- or tool-dependent;
  - "checker versus scoreboard" cites nothing (F11).

  There are also 3 attempt-first interview playgrounds.
