**Code traced by hand against the source** (no simulator is available):
- **Make It Work.**
  - Legal syntax: every name is declared; `req` and `rsp` are the built-in `uvm_driver`/`uvm_sequence` members.
  - `uvm_sequencer` uses `uvm_component_param_utils`, so `rd_sqr::type_id` exists.
  - The `response_handler` signature matches §14.2.7.3, and `set_timeout` is a `uvm_root` method.
  - Timeline: tids 1 and 2 are taken at 0, and the responses come back as 2, 1 at 10 and 4, 3 at 20, which matches the Expected output.
  - Ordering: the sequence's `wait_for_item_done` sets its ids to -1 before the driver's `item_done()` runs.
- **Break-it drills 1 to 5:**
  - drill 1: no other message;
  - drill 2: a timeout;
  - drill 3: the reused object keeps tid 1, and `expected[1]` is overwritten before each pair, so the first response of each pair matches and the second gives `RSPID`, at 10 ns and at 20 ns;
  - drill 4: `SQRPUT` at the first `put()`;
  - drill 5: the run ends at 10 ns; with a drain time, the 20 ns responses hit "Dropping response".
- **Kata.** The traced queue is correct. 10 and 9 are dropped at 50 ns, and `get_base_response(…, 9)` loops on `wait (size != queue_size)` forever.
