## Traces behind the logs (by hand; no simulator)

- **Make It Work.** Clock 10 ns (edges 5, 15, …), reset released at 20 ns (falling edge), driver leaves its reset loop at 25 ns. Each access: drive after edge k, transfer at k+1 (DUT and monitor sample with `#1step`), `item_done()` at k+2. Transfers: CTRL write 35, stray write 55 (ERR set), INT_STATUS read 75 (rdata 1), INT_STATUS write 95, CTRL read 115 ('h3), INT_STATUS read 135 ('h0), CNT read 155 (EN set at 35; increments at 45…145 = 11 = 'hb). The `W1C` info prints at 85: before 0, read 1, after 1. No check fails.
- **Kata mutants.** A: CTRL check at 125 fails ('h3 vs 'h0, fields en and led); the W1C line shows after=0; INT_STATUS passes. B: INT_STATUS check at 145 fails (read 0, mirrored 1). C: predictor toggles LED at 35, auto-predict toggles it back at 45; CTRL check fails ('h3 vs 'h1, field led). One error each.
- **Lab.** Transfers at 35 (CTRL 'h3), 55 (CFG 'h55), 75 (firmware CFG 'hA5), 95 (probe 'hFC, ERR set), 115 (IRQ read), 135 (IRQ mirror), 155 (IRQ clear), 175, 195, 215 (CFG, CTRL, IRQ mirrors). Starter (all three faults): only auto-predict runs, so the CFG check at 185 fails ('hA5 vs 'h55). After fix 1 (connect): double prediction, CTRL check at 205 fails (led read 1, mirrored 0); IRQ survives because auto-predict runs after the predictor. After fix 2 (auto-predict 0): the predictor learns 0 from the IRQ read (the monitor publishes `wdata`, which is 0 because `XreadX` sets the read item's value to 0), so the IRQ check at 145 fails (read 1, mirrored 0) while the test's own `READ` check passes. After fix 3: clean. A learner who fixes 1 and 2 together goes straight to the third symptom; step 2 covers that case.
