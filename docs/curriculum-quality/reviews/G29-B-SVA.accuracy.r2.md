**Verdict: passes. No S1 or S2 remains.** The round-1 S2 in `sva-multiclock-clock-flow` is fixed correctly. Let T0 be the source tick that launches `a`, T the attempt tick, and L the first `dst_clk` tick at or after T. The first flop captures at the first destination tick strictly after T0, which is never later than L, so `b` is sampled high no later than L + 2. With `|->`, the window `##[1:2]` checks L + 1 and L + 2. That bound is exact: a three-flop synchronizer is first sampled high at L + 3 when no destination tick falls in (T0, T), in both alignments. With `|=>` on a coincident edge, the window is L + 2 to L + 3, which is the one tick of slack the answer now states. The rubric accepts both operators when the window is justified, so the key no longer marks down the tighter answer. The round-1 S3 and S4 fixes are all in the file and correct: the goto/nonconsecutive answer says "and `beat` stays low", all four covers are unreachable, the bundle notes AXI4 `AWQOS`/`AWREGION`/`AWUSER`, and the weak/strong wording is right. ## Statements re-verified this round (no change needed)

- **Repetition** (`sva-repetition-operators`). The reference property passes only a 2-cycle pulse that rises in cycles 1–4. The traces are right: fail at 2 for the 1-cycle pulse, fail at 3 for the 3-cycle pulse, fail at 4 for the late rise. The old property passes 3- to 5-cycle pulses. The `\|-> !ack ##1 ...` variant is right. The rubric marks both `##[1:4]` forms incomplete (SVA-01 and SVA-V01 stay closed).
- **Goto and nonconsecutive** (`sva-goto-vs-nonconsecutive`). The results are right: `a_consec` fails at 3, `a_goto` fails at 6, `a_noncon` passes at 7. A fourth `beat` before `done` fails the attempt at that tick, and this holds even when the fourth beat is the very next tick. The match-item restriction matches the §16.9.2 text ("`(b, v = e)[->2]`" is illegal).
- **Interval operators** (`sva-interval-operators`). Table 16-3 order is right. `intersect` is associative, so the follow-up's parse reasoning holds. The pass at 6, fail at 4 and fail at 8 are right. `weak(seq)` ≡ `weak(first_match(seq))`.
- **Disable iff** (`sva-disable-iff-reset`). The edge-1 attempt is enabled because the synchronizer's NBA lands before Observed, while the DUT's `always_ff` read the old `rst_n` in Active. Both fixes are right. `$sampled` in a disable condition is "meaningful". `default disable iff` applies to all concurrent assertions (assert, assume, cover, restrict) in its scope (§16.15), which also confirms the "assumptions disabled too" point in `sva-formal-overconstraint`.
- **Deferred assertions** (`sva-deferred-assertions-glitch`, `sva-concurrent-vs-immediate`).
  - The `always_comb` resume is a flush point.
  - By-value arguments are evaluated with the expression; `ref` and `const ref` arguments read current values in Reactive (`#0`) or Postponed (`final`).
  - The action block is a single subroutine call.
  - Only `final` is immune to Reactive→Active loops (§16.4.1 text).
  - The time-0 `x` case is right (§9.2.2.2.2, §16.3).
- **Assert, assume, cover, restrict** (`sva-assert-assume-cover`, `sva-cover-property-vs-covergroup`).
  - An assume is checked and reported in simulation (§16.14.2), and `dist` acts as `inside` in an assert.
  - `restrict` is not verified in simulation and has no action block (§16.14.4).
  - The cover counters are right: at most one success per attempt, vacuous successes counted separately, disabled evaluations counted only as attempts (§16.14.3).
  - The latency sequence is legal and counts lengths 1 to 16.
- **Assertion control** (`sva-assertion-control`). Off leaves executing attempts and queued deferred reports alone. Kill aborts attempts and flushes them. FailOff also suppresses the default `$error`. Control types 6–11 do not change the statistics counters. `levels` defaults to 0 and follows `$dumpvars` (§20.11).
- **Bind and checkers** (`sva-bind-and-checkers`). Names resolve "as if ... at the very end of the target scope", and `$unit` declarations are not visible. An interface target takes only an interface or a checker instantiation (Syntax 23-9 footnote 4). Checker body contents and the ban on module, interface and program instances match §17.2.
- **Local variables and symbolic data** (`sva-local-variables-data-integrity`, `sva-staff-formal-strategy`, `sva-formal-convergence-signoff`). The per-attempt copies, the ticket property and the 3-bit counter bound are right. The constant free variable matches §17.7's `data_legal` example.
- **AXI** (`sva-axi-handshake-properties`, `sva-staff-formal-strategy`).
  - VALID is held and the information stays stable (A3.2.1).
  - VALID is low during reset, and the earliest drive is at a rising edge after ARESETn is HIGH (A3.1.2). So `(!ARESETn \|\| $rose(ARESETn)) \|-> !AWVALID` is right.
  - The slave can wait for AWVALID before AWREADY (A3.3.1). The 4KB rule is A3.4.1. Same-ID read ordering is A5.3.1, ID extension A5.3.5, and write-interleaving removal A5.4.
  - The liveness rule is labelled a design requirement.
- **Tool behaviour.** Every tool-specific claim is labelled tool-dependent and cited `verified: false`: pending-attempt reports, vacuity, witness and reset reports, induction engines, cut points, proof-core and mutation coverage, and lasso traces.
