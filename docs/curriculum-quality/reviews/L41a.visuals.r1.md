## Kit-level note for the lead (not an author finding)

`DiagramKit` sets `minWidth = min(width, 360)` inside `overflow-x-auto`. At a 390 px viewport, the ~600-660 px architecture and sequence diagrams are scaled to about 0.55-0.6, so their 9.5 px sublabels render at about 5.5 px. That is below README's "readable at 390 px". Every lesson that uses the kit is affected, so the fix belongs in the kit.
