# L37b accuracy review, round 1: B-AXI-4 AXI Expert Features: Cache, Prot, QoS & Atomics

Reviewer: accuracy lens, round 1. Read-only except for this file.

## Verdict: FAIL (0 S1, 2 S2, 2 S3, 10 S4)

Most of the page is accurate: every AxCACHE, AxPROT, QoS, exclusive-access, locked-transfer, AxREGION/AxUSER and atomicity claim matches IHI0022E, and every AXI5 statement matches IHI0022L. Two hands-on and assessment items contradict the sources or their own evidence, and both are S2:
- an interview answer marks a protocol-valid option wrong, and gives a false rule as the reason;
- the debug challenge's log rules out the answer it gives.
