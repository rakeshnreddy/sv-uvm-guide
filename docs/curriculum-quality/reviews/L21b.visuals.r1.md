## Findings

### S3

**V1 (S3) index.mdx:95-118 — two label collisions in the promotion diagram.**
- **Problem.** The rendered geometry has two overlaps.
  - The outer `env` container's label "ENV · env" (baseline y=32, 11 px font, so glyphs span roughly y 24-32) is crossed by the dashed top border of the `agt` container (rect top y=28, from x=28). Both containers start in row 0, and the kit places the nested container only `CONTAINER_MARGIN` (12) below the outer one, without reserving the outer label's height.
  - Inside `scb`, the port label `item_imp` (x 565 to about 608, baseline 91, 9 px) runs into the node label `scb` (centred at 606, x about 596-617, baseline 86, 11.5 px). Port and label sit at the same height because `at` defaults to 0.5.
- **Fix.** Three changes:
  - Drop the empty column inside `agt`: give `agt` `colSpan: 1` and move `chk_env` to `col: 1, colSpan: 2`, `scb` to `col: 2`, and `env` to `colSpan: 3`. This also fixes most of V2.
  - Give `scb` more room for its label, for example `cellWidth={120}`, or move the label into the caption.
  - Lead request: in `diagram-layout.ts`, reserve `CONTAINER_LABEL` above a nested container that shares its parent's first row.
  - Re-render to confirm. The two-input and worked-example diagrams have no collisions.
- **Source checked.** The props rendered via `DiagramKit.tsx` with `react-dom/server`: `<text x="26" y="32">ENV · env</text>` against `<rect x="28" y="28" …>`, and `<text x="606" y="86">scb</text>` against `<text x="565" y="91">item_imp</text>`. Also `BlockDiagram.tsx:291-300` (label position) and `:366-376` (port label position).

**V2 (S3) index.mdx:95-118 and 416-455 — the two widest diagrams are hard to read at 390 px.**
- **Problem.** The promotion diagram is 696 units wide and the worked-example diagram 640. The kit sets `minWidth = min(width, 360)`, so on a phone they scale to about 0.52 and 0.56. The 9 px port labels (`analysis_export`, `get_export`, `put_port`) then render at about 5 px. The captions carry a full text equivalent, so this is not S2. But README "Visuals" asks for diagrams that are "readable at 390 px".
- **Fix.**
  - Promotion diagram: remove the empty column (V1); the width falls to about 530.
  - Worked-example diagram: try `cellWidth={120}` and `gapX={56}`. The longest port labels (`analysis_export`, `put_export`/`get_export` on one node) still fit at 120.
  - Re-check with a 390 px viewport.
- **Source checked.** `layoutGridDiagram` widths (696, 426, 640, and 490 for the sequence diagram); `DiagramKit.tsx:50-57` (`minWidth`); `BlockDiagram.tsx:374` (`text-[9px]`).

**V3 (S3) index.mdx:452-453 — the two "down" connections in the worked-example diagram have no arrowhead.**
- **Problem.** `scb.exp_export → exp_fifo.ae` and `scb.act_export → act_fifo.ae` use `style: "structural"`. The kit draws that style as a plain line with no marker, and the legend has no entry for it. Yet the caption says "Each arrow is one connect() call, drawn from the caller to the provider". These two are exactly the export-to-child-imp ("down") connections that objective 1 teaches, and the reader cannot see their direction.
- **Fix.** Use `style: "data"`: items do flow down along the call, and the filled arrowhead then shows caller to provider like every other arrow in the figure.
- **Source checked.** `BlockDiagram.tsx:124-129` (`structural: { width: 1.2 }`, no `marker`); legend at `BlockDiagram.tsx:408-417`; `bus_scoreboard::connect_phase` (page 576-579).

**V4 (S3) index.mdx:45-53 — the symbol table omits the analysis-port glyph.**
- **Problem.** The text says "The symbols match the diagrams and visuals on this site", and the table lists `mon.ap` as an example of ■ port. But every diagram on the page draws `mon.ap`, `agt.ap`, `prod.ap` and `model.ap` as ◆, the kit's analysis-port glyph. A learner who matches symbols between table and diagram will not find ◆ in the table.
- **Fix.** Add the ◆ glyph. Either add a row (`◆` | **analysis port** | a port whose only method is `write()`, broadcast to 0..N imps | calls `connect()` like any port | `mon.ap`, `agt.ap`), or add a sentence under the table, and move `mon.ap` out of the ■ examples.
- **Source checked.** `BlockDiagram.tsx:181-189` (`analysis_port` is drawn as a diamond) and `:410-414` (legend "◆ analysis port"); page ports at 108-109, 382-383, 435, 439.

**V5 (S3) index.mdx:205-207 — the mailbox sandbox instruction does not produce the behaviour it describes.**
- **Problem.** The debrief says "then try two put() calls before any get() in the sandbox", meaning the producer should block. The sandbox opens at `new(2)` (`useState(() => freshMbx(2))`), so two `put()` calls both succeed and the learner sees no back-pressure. Nothing after the visual debriefs what it showed.
- **Fix.**
  - Change the instruction to "In the sandbox, select `new(1)`, then try two `put()` calls before any `get()`: the second waits until a consumer gets."
  - Add a one-line debrief after `<MailboxSemaphoreGame mode="mailbox" />`, for example "The blocked put resumed in the same time step as the get: that is what `put(t2)` returning at 50 ns means."
- **Source checked.** `MailboxSemaphoreGame.tsx:246` (initial bound 2), `:320-333` (bound selector), `:750-768` (mailbox mode layout).
