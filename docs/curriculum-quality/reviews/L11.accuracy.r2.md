**uvm-core 2020.3.1** (local copy `~/Downloads/1800.2-2020.3.1/src`):
- `macros/uvm_sequence_defines.svh:110-114`: `` `uvm_do(SEQ_OR_ITEM, SEQR=get_sequencer(), PRIORITY=-1, CONSTRAINTS={}) `` is `` `uvm_create `` followed by `` `uvm_rand_send ``. So `` `uvm_do(item, , , {…}) `` is a valid call, because empty actual arguments take their defaults.
- `macros/uvm_sequence_defines.svh:155-180`: `` `uvm_rand_send(SEQ_OR_ITEM, PRIORITY=-1, CONSTRAINTS={}) `` calls `start_item`, then `randomize() with`. On failure it issues `` `uvm_warning("RNDFLD", …) ``, and it calls `finish_item` in either case.
- `deprecated/macros/uvm_sequence_defines.svh:69-70, 196-197`: `` `uvm_do_with `` and `` `uvm_rand_send_with `` are wrappers over the two macros above.
- `uvm_macros.svh:95` includes the deprecated file.

## Findings

### S4-1 (optional). The `randc` restart condition drops "on that variable"

- **File:** index.mdx:146
- **Text:** "the cycle restarts whenever the constraints change"
- **Problem:** §18.4.2 says the permutation is recomputed whenever the constraints change *on that variable*, or when no remaining value satisfies them. Read alone, the sentence could suggest that any constraint change in the class restarts the cycle. The bullet at :171 and card `isv2a-20` already say "on the variable", so this is only a wording nit.
- **Fix:** "and the cycle restarts whenever the constraints on the variable change".
- **Source:** IEEE 1800-2023 §18.4.2.
