1. Semantic leading clocks and why some multiclock assertions are illegal: §16.16 rule e, §16.16.1 (set rules, the `assign clk2 = clk1` example), §16.16 rule b and example `a3`.

3. `.matched` and `.triggered` at the edges of their rules: §16.13.5 (multiple matches fork like `or`), §16.13.6 (persistence; empty matches; illegal in sampled-value functions; the unclocked `e4.triggered` example), §16.12.
