## What was verified (no finding)

**The new reset TimingDiagram (fix round 1, V3):**
- The driver's time-0 `drv_cb.valid <= 0` matures at the first `posedge` (5 ns), in the Re-NBA region after that edge's sampling. The `clk` change from X to 0 at time 0 is a negedge, not a posedge. So `valid` is sampled as `z` at 5 ns and as `0` from 15 ns.
- The request nets stay `z` until 25 ns (the §14.16 `'z` driver).
- `rst_n` reads 0, 0, 1, 1, 1 (the NBA release at 15 ns).
- The driver drives at 25 ns, the DUT accepts at 35 ns, and the second write (`1E`, address 1) is sampled at 45 ns.
- The drive at 25 ns runs after the edge's clocking event in the same time step, so it is coincident and matures in that step's Re-NBA region.
- Q1's key and explanation match.

**The drain-time-0 prediction (V10):**
- `run_phase` ends at 345 ns. At that edge the monitor has already published the read of `E` (accepted at 335 ns) and holds the read of `F` in `rd_pending`. Both the monitor and the driver resume from Observed-region triggers at 345 ns, before the drop path runs.
- Result: `MON_INFLIGHT` at 345 ns with `addr f` (`%h` of a 4-bit field), and `SCB_SUMMARY matches=15 mismatches=0 pending=0`.

**Other fix-round-1 changes:**
- The step numbers in the "One read" debrief (13 numbered messages; notes and dividers are not numbered) match the message list. "Driver already waiting" is right: `item_done()` is a function, so the driver reaches `get_next_item()` before the sequence resumes.
