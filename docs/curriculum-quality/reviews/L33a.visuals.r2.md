**Rendered check.** I rendered the real `ArchitectureDiagram` with `react-dom/server`. At 360 px the edge labels are small, about 6.6 px, but legible; that size is the kit standard noted in round 1.

- Master → slaves: HADDR, control and HWDATA, broadcast.
- Decoder → each slave: one HSELx, dotted.
