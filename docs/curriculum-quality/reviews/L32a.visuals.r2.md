## Notes for the lead (no action needed to pass)

- **Kit, 390 px.** `ArchitectureDiagram` passes `minWidth = min(width, 360)`. At 390 px the figure's inner width is about 340 px, so even these narrowed diagrams scroll about 20 px inside the figure; in the 390-px render, the accelerator's and the decoder's right edges start off-screen. Letting `minWidth` fall to about 320 px, or deriving it from the font scale, would remove the scroll on every page. The waveforms are fixed-width by design (AXI read 480 px, INCR4 392 px) and also scroll inside their figure at 390 px; there is no page-level horizontal scroll.
- **Kit, labels.** I support the author's lead request: a label-box check in `layoutGridDiagram` or `check-mdx`. A matching check in `TimingDiagram` would also help: flag a bus value whose text (`length × 6.3 + 6`) is wider than `cycleWidth`. It would have caught finding 1.
- **Kit, roles.** `BlockDiagram` and `CycleWaveform` use `role="group"`; README asks for `role="img"`. Marker labels are only exposed as `<title>`.
- The author's lead requests about the frontmatter (`order: 1`, `tier:`) and Appendix A are outside this lens and still stand.
