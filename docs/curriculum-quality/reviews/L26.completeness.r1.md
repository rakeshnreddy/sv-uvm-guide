Quick Take has What / Why / Analogy (with where it breaks), five objectives with observable verbs, and `<BeforeYouStart />`. References has `**Prerequisites:**`, Related and `<NextLesson />`.

- **Quiz.** 12 questions, each with exactly one key. Every answer has feedback, and every question has an explanation. The quiz includes predict items and debug items, and the key positions vary.
- **Deck.** 17 cards, aligned with the page; I found no contradictions.
- **Hands-on.** A kata with a worked solution, a debug challenge, and the visualizer's Debug mode.
- **Depth ladder.** Core, practitioner (Make It Work, break-it table, debug table, how-to, checklist) and 7 `### Expert:` items with sources, plus attempt-first interview playgrounds.

Generation flow is awareness-level per the card, so it has no example or picture.
