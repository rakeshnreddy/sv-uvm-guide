### One recipe, one bowl · line 17

Base line 15 · The large vessel has the eight retained bits and the spilled carry outside. A separate smaller vessel to the right holds the shift amount, and the hatched x chip shows the unknown ingredient.

> - **The Analogy:** Evaluating an expression is like a kitchen where each recipe uses one bowl size. The chef picks the bowl from the largest ingredient and from the serving dish (the **context**), pours every ingredient into that bowl, cooks, and serves into the dish, spilling whatever does not fit. Precedence is the order of the steps on the recipe card. *Where it breaks:* some ingredients are always prepared in their own small bowl, whatever the recipe (self-determined operands such as concatenation members and shift amounts), and this kitchen stocks an "unknown" ingredient, `x`, with rules of its own.

Base line 20 · The equation gives a and b. The left vessel holds eight indigo cells with 0010_1100, the separate rose cell strikes through the ninth bit, and “dropped” identifies the loss. The line underneath gives 44 >> 1 = 22 into s8. The smaller vessel is on the right and holds 1. The single x chip below carries half-width hatching so the glyph stays readable.

> A large bowl labelled 8-bit bowl, sized by a, b and the target s8, holds the sum a + b = 1100_1000 + 0110_0100. The 9-bit sum 1_0010_1100 does not fit: its carry bit is struck through and marked dropped, and the kept 8 bits 0010_1100 equal 44, which is shifted to 22 and served into s8. To the right, a small bowl holds the shift amount 1, labelled self-determined: it never sees the recipe. Below, a hatched x chip notes that one x in a or b makes the whole sum x.

Base line 68 · The rose chip sits outside the context vessel, whose eight cells and caption inside give 0010_1100 = 44. The serving line shifts 44. The small right-hand vessel holds the self-determined 1.

> **One recipe, one bowl.** The bowl is sized from the widest context-determined operand and the target, here all 8 bits, so the carry of 300 spills before the shift runs and 44 is what gets shifted. The shift amount sits in its own small bowl and never sees the recipe (§11.6.1, §11.8.2). Every width rule in this lesson is a question about this picture: how wide is the bowl, who is in it, and what spilled.

Base line 448 · The equation and serving line name the statement and stored result. The eight-bit vessel and dropped carry supply both predictions before the lab.

> Before the full trace, try the lab.
