## 3. What went wrong last time, as rules for drawing (read before every lesson)

1. **Never change a font size, a position or a viewBox without checking the neighbours.** After any such change,
   look at the visual at 390, 768, 1280 and 1440 in both themes, and run G11 and G12 for it.
2. **Text in charts must not scale with the chart.** Text drawn in viewBox units grows and shrinks with the width.
   Use `src/hooks/useSvgFontSize.ts` (added in the review) so labels render at about 11.5 px at every width, and
   keep margins wide enough for the longest label.
3. **Recolour the legend with the marks.** When a mark's colour or dash changes, its swatch, its key and any
   sentence that names its colour change in the same edit, or the mark keeps its colour.
4. **A caption is a contract.** Before redrawing a figure, copy into the report every sentence of its caption,
   `<desc>` and nearby prose that describes the drawing (a colour, dashed or solid, struck through, inside or
   outside a frame, left or right, a count, an order). After redrawing, write beside each sentence the element
   that makes it true. If a sentence cannot stay true in your design, change the design.
5. **Depth is not a border.** A part that carries the idea is a lit object: a body in its hue, a rim, a shadow.
   An outline around an empty box is not the new look. A bar chart's bars are bodies, and one of them is focal.
6. **Legibility never goes down.** If your version is harder to read than the one before it, at any width, it is
   not done. Compare with the "before" capture every time.
7. **An unchanged drawing in a new frame is not a redesign.** If an interactive's visual is drawn by the diagram
   kit (`BitStrip`, `CycleWaveform`, `BlockDiagram` and so on), you may not edit the kit, so: set what the kit
   lets you set (`focus`, roles), list what the kit would need under "Kit requests", and record the visual in the
   report as "kit-drawn, not redesigned". Do not count it as in the new look and do not wrap it to pass G1.
8. **Protected colours are not category colours.** Cyan, rose, amber, violet, indigo, teal and emerald mean what
   `docs/visual-learning/visual-language.md` says. Categories that have no protected meaning stay neutral glass
   and are told apart by label, shape or position. The fuchsia ramp is only for an ordered scale.
9. **States must read as states** in both themes: selected, active, correct, wrong, lit, dimmed.
10. **A glow must not cost the outline its contrast.** If a halo lowers the focal outline under 3:1, drop the halo
    and raise the part another way (a heavier outline, a deeper shadow).
11. **Ordered shading must read in the right direction in dark and in light.**
12. **Do not add visible words.** A title strip may show only a title the figure already had. If a figure has no
    title, it gets no strip.

## 4. Task B: the lessons, with a per-visual loop so nothing is found late

`F4A_Modules_and_Packages/index.mdx`, `F4B_Interfaces_and_Modports/index.mdx`, `F4C_Clocking_Blocks/index.mdx`
(all under `content/curriculum/T1_Foundational/`). If you finish Tier 1, continue with
`content/curriculum/T2_Intermediate/` in the order `python3 docs/curriculum-quality/workflows/figure-scan.py
--tiers T2_Intermediate` prints.

For each lesson:

1. **Capture the base** (`--capture-base`) and the "before" crops in both themes.
2. **Write the plan into the report first**: every visual, its one idea, its focal part, and the caption contract
   (rule 4). For each interactive, list every lesson that embeds it (`grep -rl "<Name" content/curriculum`).
3. **One visual at a time:** draw it, then immediately
   `node scripts/figure-gates.cjs <lesson> --base <ref> --only <visual>` and look at it at four widths in both
   themes. Fix it before you start the next visual. Do not batch visuals and gate at the end.
4. **Shared interactives:** after changing one, run `--only` for it in every lesson that embeds it, in or out of
   your list, and re-measure heights for all of them. If that breaks a released lesson, fix the component until
   the released lesson passes again.
5. **Self-review as a checklist, not prose.** In the report, one row per visual and one column per rule of
   section 3, each cell "ok" or a note with what you changed. A row may not be left blank.
6. **Full gate, then commit, then push.** `node scripts/figure-gates.cjs <lesson> --base <ref>`: all PASS (kit
   lines as `KIT NOTE`). Commit the lesson alone, `git push origin codex/figures-t1`, update the two files in
   section 5. No new entries in `scripts/figure-gates.exceptions.json`.
7. **If you are stuck on one visual for more than three attempts,** stop looping: restore that visual to its
   original, record it under "Needs a reviewer decision" with what you tried and what blocked you, mark the lesson
   "not done", and move to the next lesson. A lesson with one visual in that state is not committed as done.

Never wait idle: a height measurement or a full gate run goes in the background while you draw the next visual.
